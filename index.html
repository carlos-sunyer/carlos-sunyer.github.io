Carlos Sunyer

PhD Candidate in Finance · Universidad Carlos III de Madrid

I am a PhD Candidate in Finance at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, advised by David Martínez-Miera and Pablo Ruiz-Verdú. My research is in empirical finance, with a particular interest in financial intermediation and in the allocation of human capital — how economic and institutional forces shape who enters which professions and where they choose to work.

I am on the job market in 2026-27.

Outside of work, you will usually find me playing football, up a mountain, photographing nature, or reading essays on all sorts of subjects.
