Carlos Sunyer

PhD Candidate in Finance · Universidad Carlos III de Madrid

I am a PhD candidate in Finance at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, advised by David Martínez-Miera and Pablo Ruiz-Verdú. My research sits at the intersection of finance, labour and health economics, and studies how incentives shape the allocation of human capital — from the financial incentives that draw physicians into general practice to the effects of political polarization on where talent goes.
