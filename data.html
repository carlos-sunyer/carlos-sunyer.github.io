Lo que declaran [What They Declare]

An interactive platform to explore the asset and income declarations of Spanish members of Congress and the Senate since 2011: 2,141 parliamentarians, 6,622 declarations and more than 60,000 assets, income items and debts, compared by party, sex, age and legislature, and against the general population.
